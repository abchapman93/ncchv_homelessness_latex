\section{Introduction}

\subsection{Study population and research question}

The study population consists of Veterans who meet the following inclusion criteria:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Aged $\geq$ 18 years
    \item Contacted the National Call Center for Homeless Veterans (NCCHV) for the first time between XX/XXXX and XX/XXXX
    \item Had never enrolled in SSVF prior to their Call Center contact
    \item Were not enrolled in any VA homeless program (HUD-VASH, GPD, etc.) at the time of their Call Center contact
\end{enumerate}

Among Veterans who met these criteria and initiated SSVF within 30 days of their Call Center contact ($\Delta_A = 1$), we ask: what was the effect of SSVF initiation on their housing status 120 days after their SSVF initiation date? The primary outcome is a composite indicator of being alive and housed at 120 days: $Y(T_A + 120) = (1 - D(T_A + 120)) \cdot H(T_A + 120)$.
